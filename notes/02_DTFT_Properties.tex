DTFT: Important Properties

Use $x[n]\Leftrightarrow X(e^{j\omega})$.

Linearity

$$Ax[n]+By[n]\Leftrightarrow AX(e^{j\omega})+BY(e^{j\omega})$$

Time delay by k

$$x[n-k]\Leftrightarrow e^{-j\omega k}X(e^{j\omega})$$

Time advance by k

$$x[n+k]\Leftrightarrow e^{j\omega k}X(e^{j\omega})$$

Frequency shift / modulation

$$e^{j\omega_0n}x[n]$$

$$\Leftrightarrow X(e^{j(\omega-\omega_0)})$$

Time reversal

$$x[-n]\Leftrightarrow X(e^{-j\omega})$$

Conjugation

$$x^*[n]\Leftrightarrow X^*(e^{-j\omega})$$

Time multiplication

$$nx[n]\Leftrightarrow j\frac{dX(e^{j\omega})}{d\omega}$$

Time-domain convolution

$$y[n]=\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}x[m]h[n-m]$$

$$Y(e^{j\omega})=X(e^{j\omega})H(e^{j\omega})$$

Real-valued signal

$$X(e^{-j\omega})=X^*(e^{j\omega})$$

$$|X(e^{-j\omega})|=|X(e^{j\omega})|$$

Even real x[n]

$$x[-n]=x[n]$$

$$X(e^{j\omega})=x[0]+2\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x[n]\cos(n\omega)$$

Odd real x[n]

$$x[-n]=-x[n]$$

$$X(e^{j\omega})=-2j\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x[n]\sin(n\omega)$$

For absolutely summable odd sequences:

$$X(e^{j0})=X(e^{j\pi})=0$$

Magnitude is 2-pi periodic. For real x[n], checking $0\le\omega\le\pi$ is sufficient for the magnitude.
