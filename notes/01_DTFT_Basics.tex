DTFT: Definitions and Common Pairs

DTFT (discrete-time Fourier transform)

$$X(e^{j\omega})=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}x[n]e^{-j\omega n}$$

Inverse DTFT

$$x[n]=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}X(e^{j\omega})e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega$$

Periodicity

$$X(e^{j(\omega+2\pi)})=X(e^{j\omega})$$

Frequency is measured in radians per sample.

Unit impulse

$$\delta[n]\Leftrightarrow 1$$

Delayed unit impulse

$$\delta[n-k]\Leftrightarrow e^{-j\omega k}$$

Exponential with step ($|a|<1$)

$$a^n u[n]\Leftrightarrow\frac{1}{1-ae^{-j\omega}}$$

Matched delay

$$a^{n-k}u[n-k]$$

$$\Leftrightarrow\frac{e^{-j\omega k}}{1-ae^{-j\omega}}$$

Unmatched delay

$$a^n u[n-k]$$

$$\Leftrightarrow\frac{a^k e^{-j\omega k}}{1-ae^{-j\omega}}$$

Exponential cut to N samples

$$x[n]=a^n(u[n]-u[n-N])$$

$$X(e^{j\omega})=\frac{1-a^Ne^{-j\omega N}}{1-ae^{-j\omega}}$$

Valid for complex $a$, except when the denominator is zero; at those points use the finite-sum limit.

Multiplying x[n] by C multiplies its DTFT by C.

Example: $\left(\frac12\right)^{n-1}=2\left(\frac12\right)^n$.
