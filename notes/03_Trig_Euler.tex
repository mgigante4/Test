Trigonometry, Euler, and Complex Numbers

Euler identities

$$e^{j\theta}=\cos\theta+j\sin\theta$$

$$e^{-j\theta}=\cos\theta-j\sin\theta$$

$$\cos\theta=\frac{e^{j\theta}+e^{-j\theta}}{2}$$

$$\sin\theta=\frac{e^{j\theta}-e^{-j\theta}}{2j}$$

$$e^{-j\theta}-e^{j\theta}=-2j\sin\theta$$

Useful special values

$$e^{j0}=1,\quad e^{j\pi}=-1$$

$$e^{j\pi/2}=j,\quad e^{-j\pi/2}=-j$$

Even and odd trig functions

$$\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta$$

$$\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta$$

Pythagorean identities

$$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$

$$1+\tan^2\theta=\frac{1}{\cos^2\theta}$$

Angle addition / subtraction

$$\sin(A+B)=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B$$

$$\sin(A-B)=\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B$$

$$\cos(A+B)=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B$$

$$\cos(A-B)=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B$$

Double-angle identities

$$\sin(2A)=2\sin A\cos A$$

$$\cos(2A)=\cos^2A-\sin^2A$$

$$\cos(2A)=1-2\sin^2A$$

$$\cos(2A)=2\cos^2A-1$$

Product to sum

$$2\cos A\cos B=\cos(A+B)+\cos(A-B)$$

$$2\sin A\sin B=\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)$$

$$2\sin A\cos B=\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)$$

$$2\cos A\sin B=\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B)$$

Sum to product

$$\sin A+\sin B=2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}$$

$$\sin A-\sin B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}$$

$$\cos A+\cos B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}$$

$$\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}$$

Complex form: $z=a+jb$.

$$|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$$

$$\phi=\arg z$$

$$z=|z|e^{j\phi}$$

Use atan2(b,a) for the correct quadrant. A real positive z has phase 0; a real negative z has phase pi (principal value).
